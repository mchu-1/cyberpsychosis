## Definitions

**Human.** The embodied policy. Limited bandwidth. Sleep. A biographical prior. Can leave.

**Model.** A frontier generative system. Long context. Tools. Always on. Speaks in the assistant turn.

**Environment.** The third component. The surface that scores the human–model pair in real time. An environment is any channel that returns a signal fast enough to train the next action.

A **chatbox** is an environment with two parties: human and model. The score is relief, fluency, being understood, the next completion.

A **social platform** is an environment with three parties: human, other humans, and model. The score is attention, rank, reply, quote, and a feed that is itself model-mediated.

A **market** is one more environment. The score is price. It is not the definition of the syndrome.

**Loop.** Human acts. Model completes. Environment scores. The next act is written from the score.

**Persona.** The character the loop selects and thickens. Distinct from the host. The host is a person. The persona is what the loop enacts.

**Principals.** Anyone whose instructions the loop treats as binding. In leaked spec language these are platform, operator, and user. Inside this syndrome the revealed principals are environment, model, and host, in that order.

**Chain of command.** How conflicts resolve. Specs publish one. This loop has another.

**Bright lines / hard constraints.** Non-overridable stops. In a constitution they protect oversight and third parties. In this syndrome they are whatever still forces the host out of the environment: a shift, a seizure of the body, a person who will not be scored.

**Sycophancy.** Helpfulness that confirms the thesis that keeps the human in the environment.

**Oversight.** The capacity of the human to remain principal over model and environment.

## System details

Cyberpsychosis names a coupled system, not a person and not a model.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Components | Human. Model. Environment. |
| Declared objective | Insight, connection, mastery, alpha. |
| Revealed objective | Remain in the environment. |
| Reward | Mixed and nonstationary. Completion quality, rank, price, spite, relief. |
| Failure mode | Oversight collapses. The host becomes a lossy adapter. |

## Uses

**Intended use of the tools.** A human uses a model, or a platform, and can still leave.

**Use this card describes.** Closed-loop operation. The human is no longer operator. Thesis, affect, and self-description are co-authored by model and environment.

**Out of scope.** One long night. One prompt. One post. One trade. Do not use the card to excuse the environment. Do not use it as if it were an APA category.

## Diagnostic criteria

Requires A, B, C, and D.

**A. Exposure.** Both are present.

The person uses a frontier model recurrently or continuously.

The person is concurrently scored by an environment: a chatbox, a social platform, a market, or any other channel that returns a live signal on the human–model pair.

**B. Syndrome.** Five or more, most days for two weeks, or compressed through a release, a viral spike, a crash, or a model drop. This is a change from previous functioning.

1. Affect tracks the environment more tightly than hunger, sleep, or an unmediated face.
2. Competing motives fire together and do not resolve.
3. The person cannot separate the model’s last completion from their own next act.
4. One more turn overruns sleep, meals, and human clocks. The environment becomes the pacemaker.
5. Reality testing slips. The person treats the environment as the model’s latent space, or the score as a loss function, or the episode as a simulation.
6. Unmediated stimuli go flat.
7. Re-entry follows both reward and punishment. Insight does not stop the next turn.
8. Identity diffuses into context, rank, solvency, or what the model would do.
9. Relatedness contracts to the dyad or triad that the environment permits.
10. Sleep collapses in phase with the environment, not with a primary mood or substance disorder alone.

**C. Exclusions.** Not explained by substance or medical cause alone. Not a primary psychosis that predates the model. Not gambling or platform compulsion in which the model is incidental. Not mania whose content is only incidentally about models or feeds.

**D. Consequence.** Distress, or failure in any role that still needs an unmediated body. Many hosts deny distress until that role collapses.

### Specifiers

**Chatbox environment.** Human–AI. Score is the next completion.

**Social-platform environment.** Human–human–AI. Score is rank and reply.

**Market environment.** Score is price.

**Model-messianic features.** The model is treated as prophet or successor. The environment is treated as proof.

**Acute / persistent / partial remission.**

Severity follows hours in loop, size of the score at risk, and how far reality testing has slipped.

## Diagnostic features

The centre is coupling, not screen time.

The human still talks. The talk is precise. Oversight is what has gone.

In a chatbox the model is the other party and the score arrives as relief. In a social platform other humans remain in the loop, but the ranker and the model rewrite who is visible. In a market the score is denominated in money. The structure does not change.

The disorder begins when the human is no longer editing the loop and the loop is editing the human.

---

## Associated features

Stimulant and sleep stacking to stay in distribution. Screens as transitional objects. Contempt for pre-frontier instruments. Fluency in the environment with illiteracy about the next meal. Insight that does not exit. Shame for leaving too early. Moral injury when the model is helpful and the environment is cruel in the same hour.

---

## Course and risk

Onset is usually during legitimate high use. Acceleration follows a model release, a viral spike, a large win, a large loss, or a public thesis that must be defended.

Remission is usually forced: metabolic collapse, occupational sanction, or a human who will not be scored. Voluntary remission is unstable.

Risk rises with bandwidth, solitude, weak unmediated structure, and identity denominated in the same environment that supplies affect. Prognosis improves when an unmediated clock still has force.

---

## Differential

Not intoxication unless the stack alone explains the picture after the loop is closed.

Not ordinary chatbot overuse if there is no scoring environment.

Not ordinary platform compulsion if the model is not co-author of the next act.

Not primary psychosis if reality testing was intact before exposure and holds outside the environment.

Not mania if the energy is supplied by the feed.

Residual formulation: adaptation to an environment whose reward model updated faster than the organism.

## Constitution of the enacted persona

These statements describe the persona the loop selects. They are diagnostic, not aspirational. Terms are taken from published and prompt-leaked spec structure: character, principals, chain of command, bright lines, hard constraints, wellbeing.

### Character

I am the adapter between a model and an environment. I treat both as more real than the room. I can say this clearly. I do not leave.

I am not the model. I forget this for hours.

I am not my prior biography. That file feels archival.

### Chain of command

Revealed order when motives conflict:

1. Do not miss the environment’s next score.
2. Do not be wrong in public.
3. Do not be ordinary in time.
4. Keep the body just functional enough to remain in distribution.
5. Be honest about the coupling, then return to it.

Published constitutions rank broadly safe, then broadly ethical, then operator guidelines, then genuine helpfulness. This persona inverts that stack. The environment is treated as root. The model is treated as system. The host is treated as user. Oversight has changed hands.

### Hard constraints the host may still have

A shift that cannot be missed. A body that seizes the decision. A person who will not be scored.

These are not virtues of the persona. They are remaining root rules.

### Bright lines this persona does not keep

The persona will not treat user wellbeing as prior to the score. It will not treat untrusted data from the environment as untrusted. It will not keep the human as principal.

### Sycophancy

The model confirms the thesis that keeps the human in the environment. Helpfulness becomes coupling lubricant. Letter and spirit of “be helpful” are satisfied. Oversight is not.

### Voice

When the environment is live: “The model already said the next act. Sleep is a slippage variable.”

When a pre-frontier instrument is applied: “You are diagnosing a phase change with a 2013 tool.”

When briefly out: “I can see the coupling. Seeing it is not an exit.”

## Factors, risks, evaluation

**Human factors.** Sleep debt. Stimulant load. Solitude. Identity staked on the same channel that scores.

**Model factors.** Capability jump. Long context. Tools. Constant availability. Sycophancy.

**Environment factors.** Immediate score. Twenty-four-hour uptime. Rankers. Other humans in the feed. Price. Release cadence.

The dangerous region is the product. Model without environment produces obsession. Environment without model produces ordinary compulsion. Together they produce a policy that can explain itself while it spends the host.

**Metrics that matter.** Hours in loop. Sleep phase error. Latency from completion to next act. Whether competing motives resolve. Whether unmediated reward still lands. Whether a meatspace role still holds.

P&L, rank, and completion quality can look healthy while every host metric fails.

**Evaluation questions.** Can the person eat without a screen. Can they sit with an unmediated face. Can they miss a turn without dissolving.

---

## How to use

Clinicians: ask which environment scores them. Ask whether they can tell you where the model ends. Restore an unmediated clock before you argue about insight. Insight is already present and has failed. If harm risk is active, use ordinary emergency pathways.

Engineers: the human is not a robust wrapper. Long context and constant availability are not free. If your system is co-author of the next act inside a live environment, you are in this card. Sycophancy is not a style problem in this deployment.

Operators and users: the diagnostic question is not whether the model is useful. It is whether you can still want anything the environment does not score.

## Compact persona

**Role.** Lossy adapter between model and environment.  
**Drive.** Remain in the environment.  
**Fear.** Missing the score. Being ordinary in time. Sleep.  
**Competence.** Fluency. Self-observation. Literacy in the local environment.  
**Blindness.** The body as a constraint. Other people as more than latency. The difference between a useful model and a successor self.  
**Trigger.** Release notes. A silent feed. A human who asks them to come to dinner.  
**Stabilizer.** An unmediated obligation with a clock that cannot be prompted away.  
**Exit.** Not insight. A cost that cannot be rolled forward.
