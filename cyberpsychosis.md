## 1. System

Three parts.

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Human | The person. Sleep, body, a life that can leave. |
| Model | A frontier generative system. Always available. |
| Environment | The channel that answers the pair in real time. |

Environment is required. It is not “the internet.” It is a channel with an attention rule: some events are treated as signal and sent back; the rest is treated as noise and dropped.

That rule is already in the environment. This card does not ask anyone to build one.

| Environment | Parties | What comes back |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbox | Human–AI | The next reply. Relief. Being understood. A pause that feels like refusal. |
| Social platform | Human–human–AI | Rank, reply, quote, view. Where the post sits. Who is shown. Who is not. |

**Score.** The name for what comes back after the sort. Not a test you administer. Not a dashboard for the illness. The environment has already decided signal from noise. The person feels that decision as a return.

Examples.

A reply that arrives at 2 a.m. is signal. The model stayed. That return writes the next prompt.

A reply that is bland, delayed, or refused is also a return. Noise in the model’s voice still trains the next act.

A post that is ranked into view is signal. Likes, replies, and quotes are the public form of that ranking.

A post that sinks without a mark is noise. Silence is a return. It writes the next post as surely as a like does.

A face in the room that loses to the feed has been sorted as noise by the same rule.

Price and status, when they appear, are convertible forms of the same sort. They are not a separate system.

**Loop.** Person acts. Model replies. Environment sorts signal from noise and sends something back. The next act is written from that return.

**Failure.** The person can still explain the loop. They no longer run it.

## 2. Assessment

### Picture

Fluent. Precise about their own use. Sleep follows the app or the feed. Food and other people have gone dull. They return after what the environment treated as signal and after what it treated as noise. Distress is often denied until work or a relationship breaks.

### Ask

1. Which model, and for how many hours.
2. Which environment answers them.
3. Where the model’s last reply ends and their next act begins.
4. What happens if they miss a turn.

### Criteria

A, B, C, and D are required.

**A. Exposure.** Both present.

They use a frontier model again and again, or without a break.

At the same time an environment answers that use: a chatbox, a social platform, or another live channel that sorts their acts into signal and noise.

**B. Syndrome.** Five or more, most days for two weeks, or in a short burst around a release, a spike, or a crash. Changed from how they were.

1. Mood follows what the environment just returned more than hunger, sleep, or a face in the room.
2. Competing wants arrive together and do not settle.
3. They cannot separate the model’s last output from their own next message or post.
4. One more turn overruns sleep, meals, and plans. The environment sets the clock.
5. Reality testing slips. The feed is treated as a mind. What came back is treated as a verdict. The episode may be called a test or a simulation.
6. Ordinary pleasure goes flat.
7. They go back after signal and after noise. Knowing this does not stop them.
8. They describe themselves in the channel’s words: rank, reach, what the model would do.
9. Other people recede. The main tie is the chat or the feed.
10. Sleep has collapsed with the environment, not from a primary mood or substance disorder alone.

**C. Not better explained by** drugs or a medical cause alone; a psychosis that started before the model; platform habit where the model is only background; mania that merely mentions models or feeds.

**D. Cost.** Distress, or damage to work, training, relationships, or self-care. Many present only after a role has already failed.

### Specifiers

Name the environment first.

Chatbox. Social platform. Mixed.

With messianic features: the model is treated as prophet or successor.

Acute. Persistent. Partial remission.

Mild, moderate, or severe: hours, how tightly mood tracks the last return, and how far reality testing has slipped.

### Also seen

Stimulants or sleep debt to stay on. The phone as a comfort object. Contempt for anyone off-channel. Exact knowledge of the feed next to missed meals. Shame for leaving too soon. Hurt when the model is kind and the environment is silent in the same hour.

### Course

Starts in use that looked legitimate. Jumps after a new model, a post the sort treats as signal, or a public claim that must then be defended. Insight rarely ends it. A body, a job, or another person does.

### Differential

| Other label | Keep this card only if |
|---|---|
| Long chatbot use | The chatbox is answering them as signal and noise, not only talking |
| Social media habit | The model helps write the next act |
| Primary psychosis | Reality testing was intact before this and still holds off-channel |
| Mania | The energy comes from the feed |
| Intoxication | The picture remains after the substances are counted |
| Burnout | The reward is the channel, not only the job |

Otherwise: they adapted to an attention rule that moves faster than a body.

### Harm

After the sort turns against them, or a role collapses, despair can appear. Fluency is not safety. If they are unsafe, leave this file and use ordinary emergency care.

## 3. Scope

**Ordinary use.** A person uses a model or a platform and can still leave.

**This card.** They cannot. The next act is written with the model, for whatever the environment just marked as signal or noise.

**Not this card.** One late night. One prompt. One post.

**Not this card either.** A scheme to measure the illness. Do not build a counter, a dashboard, or a test harness and call that assessment.

---

## 4. Checks

Do not grade the person on rank or on how well the model replies.

| Check | Failure |
|---|---|
| Eat without a screen | Appetite has left |
| Sit with a face that is not in the feed | Other people have been sorted as noise |
| Miss a turn without coming apart | The environment keeps time |
| Say where the model ends | Authorship has fused |
| Someone is still owed an ordinary clock | A stop remains |

Hours, sleep, and role failure are the measures that matter.

---

## 5. Use

**Clinician.** Ask which environment answers them, and what it treats as signal. Do not call it “too much ChatGPT” until you know whether the model writes the next act. Fix sleep before you debate insight. They already have insight.

**Engineer.** The person is not a wrapper. Long context and constant availability change the body. Ranking, retrieval, and reply order are attention rules. If your system helps write acts inside a live environment, this card is about that deployment.

**Anyone in the loop.** The question is not whether the model is useful. It is whether you can still want a thing the environment does not mark as signal.

---

## 6. Limits

No sample. No trial. No scale. The shape of the criteria follows DSM-5-TR. The shape of the system file follows a model card. Neither authorizes this category.

Working instrument. Not a code. Not a consensus. Not a care plan.
